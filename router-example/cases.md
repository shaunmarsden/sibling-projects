# Fictional Cases: Two Routing Decisions

Two entirely fictional situations, each built to test a confusion pair from [ROUTER.md](../ROUTER.md).

## Case A

"My friend keeps saying she'll think about joining our book club, but it's been six weeks now and she hasn't actually said no. Starting to feel like this isn't going to happen. What should I do?"

## Case B

"Three separate customers have mentioned slow loading times in their reviews of our app. Is this actually something to fix, or am I reading too much into a couple of similar-sounding complaints?"
