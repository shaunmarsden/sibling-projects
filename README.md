# Sibling Projects

A list of tool ideas. Each one takes a pattern that already works in [practical-ai-sales-workflows](https://github.com/shaunmarsden/practical-ai-sales-workflows) or [book-to-skill](https://github.com/shaunmarsden/book-to-skill) and opens it up beyond sales. By 22 August 2026 I'd built all 19. Each is a real `SKILL.md` with a made-up worked example full of deliberate traps, not just a description. This file is the index, and the record of what each tool comes from and why.

**Not sure which one fits your situation?** [The router](ROUTER.md) reads a plain description of your problem and points you to the right one of the 19. It checks the pairs that sound alike but aren't, such as a closed case against one still stalled, or the parts of one decision against a pattern across several cases. Paste it into any AI chat tool, then go through [the review checklist](checks/checklist.md) before you act on what it says.

Torn between two tools that sound alike, and on the web rather than in an AI chat? [The interactive picker](https://shaunmarsden.github.io/sibling-projects/) covers six of those pairs as cards you can click. For anything else, use the router or browse the list below.

## Strongest, Most Differentiated

1. [Evidence-Labelled Meeting Notes](ideas/01-evidence-labelled-meeting-notes.md): keep confirmed facts, estimates and assumptions apart in any meeting write-up, not just a sales call. [Repo](https://github.com/shaunmarsden/evidence-labelled-meeting-notes)
2. [Build Your Own AI Output Rubric](ideas/02-build-your-own-rubric.md): build a rubric for scoring AI output in any field, not just sales. [Repo](https://github.com/shaunmarsden/build-your-own-rubric)
3. [Skill Author](ideas/03-skill-author.md): turn a task you repeat into a proper skill with clear limits. It's the tool behind everything else on this list. [Repo](https://github.com/shaunmarsden/skill-author)

## Solid, Narrower Generalisations

4. [Claims vs. Evidence Checker](ideas/04-claims-vs-evidence-checker.md): check whether the evidence backs up a status, in any tracker. [Repo](https://github.com/shaunmarsden/claims-vs-evidence-checker)
5. [List Hygiene Checker](ideas/05-list-hygiene-checker.md): find duplicates, missing fields and stale rows in any spreadsheet or list. [Repo](https://github.com/shaunmarsden/list-hygiene-checker)
6. [Post-Mortem Builder](ideas/06-post-mortem-builder.md): work out whether a failed effort is really over, not just a lost sale. [Repo](https://github.com/shaunmarsden/post-mortem-builder)
7. [The Quiet Follow-Up](ideas/07-the-quiet-follow-up.md): decide what to send, if anything, when someone has gone quiet, not just a sales prospect. [Repo](https://github.com/shaunmarsden/the-quiet-follow-up)

## More Speculative

8. [Diagnose Before You Respond](ideas/08-diagnose-before-you-respond.md): find the real reason behind pushback before you answer it. I limited it to customer complaints. [Repo](https://github.com/shaunmarsden/diagnose-before-you-respond)
9. [Course/Policy to Skill](ideas/09-course-policy-to-skill.md): book-to-skill's method, aimed at training material or a policy document instead of a book. I built it as a second worked example inside [book-to-skill](https://github.com/shaunmarsden/book-to-skill/tree/main/example-two), not as its own repo.

## Round Two

Four more, taken from patterns in the sales repo that the first nine hadn't covered:

10. [Is This Actually a Good Fit?](ideas/10-is-this-a-good-fit.md): sort each part of a decision into good fit, poor fit or uncertain, not just for a sales prospect. [Repo](https://github.com/shaunmarsden/is-this-a-good-fit)
11. [Brief Your Advocate](ideas/11-brief-your-advocate.md): prepare someone to make your case to a third person when you can't be in the room. [Repo](https://github.com/shaunmarsden/brief-your-advocate)
12. [Make the Case](ideas/12-make-the-case.md): build or check a written case for a decision-maker who wasn't in the earlier conversations. [Repo](https://github.com/shaunmarsden/make-the-case)
13. [Weekly Roundup Builder](ideas/13-weekly-roundup-builder.md): pull what you have this week into one honest report, with no made-up trends. [Repo](https://github.com/shaunmarsden/weekly-roundup-builder)

## Round Three

14. [What's Actually Causing This Delay?](ideas/14-whats-causing-this-delay.md): work out whether a stalled decision is real indecision, an unspoken concern, a real dependency or a soft no. Post-Mortem Builder is for cases already closed; this one is for a case still open. [Repo](https://github.com/shaunmarsden/whats-causing-this-delay)

## Round Four

15. [Is This Really a Pattern?](ideas/15-is-this-really-a-pattern.md): in any log of complaints, feedback or incidents, tell a real repeated cause apart from similar wording that hides different problems. [Repo](https://github.com/shaunmarsden/is-this-really-a-pattern)

## Round Five

16. [Personalise, Don't Templatise](ideas/16-personalise-dont-templatise.md): draft a message from a fixed template with real personal detail, and handle several recipients or a message forwarded to someone else. [Repo](https://github.com/shaunmarsden/personalise-dont-templatise)

## Round Six

17. [Prep Card Builder](ideas/17-prep-card-builder.md): turn scattered notes into a short card, based on evidence, to scan before any important conversation. [Repo](https://github.com/shaunmarsden/prep-card-builder)

## Round Seven

18. [First Contact That Isn't Generic](ideas/18-first-contact-that-isnt-generic.md): pick who to contact cold and draft a first message worth replying to, built on a real signal. It was the last sales-repo pattern I hadn't used. [Repo](https://github.com/shaunmarsden/first-contact-that-isnt-generic)

## Round Eight

19. [Do These Actually Match?](ideas/19-do-these-actually-match.md): compare two separately kept records of the same thing and show only where they really disagree. It comes from the Conflicting evidence label in the sales repo's approval-gated copilot guide, not from a particular sales skill. [Repo](https://github.com/shaunmarsden/do-these-actually-match)

## All Nineteen Built

Every idea on this list now has its own repo, except #9, which worked better as a second worked example inside an existing one. Each brief above still records what the tool comes from and the questions I had before building it. Every tool now also has a second worked example that tests a different case from its first. For [Evidence-Labelled Meeting Notes](ideas/01-evidence-labelled-meeting-notes.md), it tests the opposite failure.
