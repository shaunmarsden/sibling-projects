# Prep Card Builder

Turn scattered information into a short card, based on evidence, to scan before any important conversation: an interview, a negotiation, a hard family conversation, a professional appointment.

It comes from [prepare-for-sales-call](https://github.com/shaunmarsden/practical-ai-sales-workflows/blob/main/.agents/skills/prepare-for-sales-call/SKILL.md), with everything sales-specific taken out.

It's for anyone going into an important conversation with scattered notes and public background, who wants confirmed facts, public context and open questions kept honestly apart rather than blurred into false confidence.

I built it on 3 August 2026. [The repo](https://github.com/shaunmarsden/prep-card-builder) has a made-up job interview example. It rightly keeps a candidate's public LinkedIn posts apart from confirmed evidence of the skill being assessed.
