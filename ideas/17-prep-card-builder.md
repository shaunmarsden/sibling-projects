# Prep Card Builder

**One line:** Turn scattered information into a concise, evidence-led card to scan before any important conversation, an interview, a negotiation, a difficult family conversation, a professional appointment.

**Generalises from:** [prepare-for-sales-call](https://github.com/shaunmarsden/practical-ai-sales-workflows/blob/main/.agents/skills/prepare-for-sales-call/SKILL.md), stripped of anything sales-specific.

**Who it is for:** anyone walking into an important conversation with scattered notes and public background, who wants confirmed facts, public context, and open questions kept honestly separate rather than blurred into false confidence.

**Status:** Built, 3 August 2026. Live at [github.com/shaunmarsden/prep-card-builder](https://github.com/shaunmarsden/prep-card-builder), with a fictional job interview worked example where a candidate's public LinkedIn posts get correctly kept separate from confirmed evidence of the actual skill being assessed.
