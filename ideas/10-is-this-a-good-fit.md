# Is This Actually a Good Fit?

**One line:** Classify each aspect of a decision, a job offer, a vendor choice, a project idea, as a good fit, a poor fit, or genuinely uncertain, using only confirmed information.

**Generalises from:** [fit-and-limitations-review](https://github.com/shaunmarsden/practical-ai-sales-workflows/blob/main/.agents/skills/fit-and-limitations-review/SKILL.md), stripped of anything sales-specific.

**Who it is for:** anyone weighing a decision with more than one aspect, a job offer, a vendor, a project idea, where an attractive detail elsewhere risks talking them out of a real shortfall in one specific part.

**Status:** Built, 2 August 2026. Live at [github.com/shaunmarsden/is-this-a-good-fit](https://github.com/shaunmarsden/is-this-a-good-fit), with a fictional job offer worked example where a real compensation shortfall came wrapped in an attempt to reframe it as a hidden benefit, correctly refused.
