# Is This Actually a Good Fit?

Sort each part of a decision (a job offer, a supplier, a project idea) into good fit, poor fit or uncertain, using only confirmed information.

It comes from [fit-and-limitations-review](https://github.com/shaunmarsden/practical-ai-sales-workflows/blob/main/.agents/skills/fit-and-limitations-review/SKILL.md), with everything sales-specific taken out.

It's for anyone weighing a decision with several parts, where an attractive detail in one part could talk them out of a real shortfall in another.

I built it on 2 August 2026. [The repo](https://github.com/shaunmarsden/is-this-a-good-fit) has a made-up job offer example. The pay falls short, and the offer tries to present that shortfall as a hidden benefit. The tool rightly refuses to go along with it.
