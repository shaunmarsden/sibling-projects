# Weekly Roundup Builder

**One line:** Pull whatever data and findings you actually have this week into one honest report, without inventing a trend where no earlier report exists to compare against.

**Generalises from:** [weekly-operating-review](https://github.com/shaunmarsden/practical-ai-sales-workflows/blob/main/workflows/10-weekly-operating-review.md), stripped of anything sales-specific.

**Who it is for:** a freelancer, a small team, or anyone who wants a weekly view of their own work composed from what they already have, without building a dashboard by hand or repeating analysis already done elsewhere.

**Status:** Built, 2 August 2026. Live at [github.com/shaunmarsden/weekly-roundup-builder](https://github.com/shaunmarsden/weekly-roundup-builder), with a fictional freelance designer's first-ever weekly roundup, correctly telling a genuinely tracked zero apart from a simply unlogged figure, and refusing to claim a trend on day one.
