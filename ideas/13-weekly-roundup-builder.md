# Weekly Roundup Builder

Pull whatever data and findings you have this week into one honest report, without inventing a trend when there's no earlier report to compare with.

It comes from [weekly-operating-review](https://github.com/shaunmarsden/practical-ai-sales-workflows/blob/main/workflows/10-weekly-operating-review.md), with everything sales-specific taken out.

It's for a freelancer, a small team, or anyone who wants a weekly view of their own work built from what they already have, without building a dashboard by hand or repeating analysis done elsewhere.

I built it on 2 August 2026. [The repo](https://github.com/shaunmarsden/weekly-roundup-builder) has a made-up freelance designer's first weekly roundup. It rightly tells a tracked zero apart from a figure nobody logged, and refuses to claim a trend on day one.
