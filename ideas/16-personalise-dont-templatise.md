# Personalise, Don't Templatise

**One line:** Draft a fixed-template message personalised with real detail, correctly handling a single recipient, more than one recipient at once, or a recipient forwarding part of it to someone who was not there.

**Generalises from:** [draft-follow-up-email](https://github.com/shaunmarsden/practical-ai-sales-workflows/blob/main/.agents/skills/draft-follow-up-email/SKILL.md), stripped of anything sales-specific.

**Who it is for:** anyone sending the same template repeatedly, a teacher's parent updates, an event organiser's attendee follow-ups, HR's onboarding emails, where the template should stay fixed and only the personalisation changes.

**Status:** Built, 3 August 2026. Live at [github.com/shaunmarsden/personalise-dont-templatise](https://github.com/shaunmarsden/personalise-dont-templatise), with a fictional photography workshop's follow-up template sent to a single recipient, two recipients together, and a forward-to-someone-else case, all three correctly handled without the template itself changing.
