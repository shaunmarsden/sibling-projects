# What's Actually Causing This Delay?

Work out whether someone's delay on a decision is real indecision, an unspoken concern, a real wait for someone else's approval, or a soft no, before you decide how to respond.

It comes from [identify-buyer-indecision](https://github.com/shaunmarsden/practical-ai-sales-workflows/blob/main/.agents/skills/identify-buyer-indecision/SKILL.md), with everything sales-specific taken out.

It's for anyone waiting on a decision that has stalled but not closed: a manager sitting on an approval, a landlord putting off a lease, anyone whose "let me think about it" has gone on too long. It pairs with [post-mortem-builder](https://github.com/shaunmarsden/post-mortem-builder), which is for cases already closed; this one is for a case still open.

I built it on 2 August 2026. [The repo](https://github.com/shaunmarsden/whats-causing-this-delay) has a made-up example of three stalled approvals at work. The tool rightly tells them apart as real indecision, a real wait for approval, and a soft no, each needing a different response.
