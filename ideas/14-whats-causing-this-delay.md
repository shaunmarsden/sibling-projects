# What's Actually Causing This Delay?

**One line:** Diagnose whether someone's ongoing delay on a decision is genuine indecision, an unstated concern, a real approval dependency, or a soft no, before deciding how to respond.

**Generalises from:** [identify-buyer-indecision](https://github.com/shaunmarsden/practical-ai-sales-workflows/blob/main/.agents/skills/identify-buyer-indecision/SKILL.md), stripped of anything sales-specific.

**Who it is for:** anyone waiting on a stalled decision that has not closed one way or another yet, a manager sitting on an approval, a landlord deferring on a lease, anyone whose "let me think about it" has gone on longer than it should. Complements [post-mortem-builder](https://github.com/shaunmarsden/post-mortem-builder), which is for cases that have already closed; this is for one still open.

**Status:** Built, 2 August 2026. Live at [github.com/shaunmarsden/whats-causing-this-delay](https://github.com/shaunmarsden/whats-causing-this-delay), with a fictional worked example of three stalled workplace approvals, correctly told apart as genuine indecision, a real approval dependency, and a soft no, each needing a genuinely different response.
