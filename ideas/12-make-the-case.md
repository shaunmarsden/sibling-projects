# Make the Case

**One line:** Build or audit a written case for a decision-maker who was not part of the original conversations, a funding request, a headcount ask, a proposal to a committee.

**Generalises from:** [build-business-case](https://github.com/shaunmarsden/practical-ai-sales-workflows/blob/main/.agents/skills/build-business-case/SKILL.md), stripped of anything sales-specific.

**Who it is for:** anyone building a proper written case for someone who was not in the room, a community group applying for a grant, an employee making an internal ask, anyone whose request needs to stand on its own without them there to argue it live.

**Status:** Built, 2 August 2026. Live at [github.com/shaunmarsden/make-the-case](https://github.com/shaunmarsden/make-the-case), with a fictional community garden group's greenhouse funding request, where the actual cost was still unconfirmed and correctly left as an open placeholder rather than an estimated number.
